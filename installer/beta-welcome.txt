Robin Control Lite — Moonbase Beta

Private installation and activation test build, version 2.0.0.
For macOS 11 or later on Apple silicon and Intel Macs.

Choose VST3, Audio Unit (AU), and/or AAX Native for Pro Tools.
Close your audio applications before installing, then reopen your host and
load Robin Control Lite. Follow Activate online to activate your free RCL
license through conduit.dsp's Moonbase account. Offline file activation is
also available.

Online activations have a 90-day offline grace period since their last
successful online validation, subject to signed license expiry/revocation.
Permanent offline activations are separate. The current product configuration
allows 10 activations per license. An RCL entitlement must exist in the account
used for activation; installing this beta does not issue a license.

This build connects to Moonbase for licensing and stores a signed license
locally. Optional analytics are disabled. Samples and presets stay local.

This is a private test build, not the public 2.0.0 release.
Report installation, activation, and playback results to hello@conduitdsp.com.
