Robin Control Lite 2.0.0 - Moonbase Windows Beta 1

This private beta installs the Windows x64 VST3 plugin.

After installation, rescan plugins in your DAW and open Robin Control Lite.
Use Activate to sign in with a Moonbase account that owns Robin Control Lite,
or use the supported offline activation flow. Playback requires activation.

This is a test build, not the final public release. Please test sample loading,
MIDI playback, Trigger/Panic, and saving and reopening a DAW session.
